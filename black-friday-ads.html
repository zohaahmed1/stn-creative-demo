Questions we get asked.

What counts as one ad?

One finished creative, sized for where it runs. That can be a static, a short video or a UGC-style ad.

Which platforms do you make them for?

Meta, TikTok and Pinterest. Snapchat too if you're running there.

What do you need from us?

Product photos or links, your brand guidelines, and any past ads that did well. We take it from there.

How do you make 10 a week?

We use AI for the heavy lifting, and our team directs every ad. That's how the volume stays up without the quality dropping.

Do you run the ads too?

We can. Media buying is something we do as well, so we can talk about it on the call.

Is it too late to start?

Not yet. Every week you start earlier is 10 more ads tested before sale week.

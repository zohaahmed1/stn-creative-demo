Before you book

Questions we get on every call.

What if I already have an agency?

Most of our clients came from another agency. You can keep them running while we audit, or use the 3 free ad concepts to stress-test what you're getting today. We don't ask for a switch until we've shown the work is better.

Do you work with brands under $30k/mo in ad spend?

We work best with brands spending $30k+/mo per platform. Under that, our production volume and media fees don't pencil out for you. If you're smaller, book the call anyway — we'll send the 3 ad concepts and point you to the right next step.

What happens if we don't hit the 45-day guarantee?

We work for free until we do. The guarantee is tied to ROAS improvement (DTC) or CPL / pipeline lift (SaaS) benchmarked against your trailing 30-day baseline before we start. No fine print.

What does week 1 actually look like?

Day 1–2: knowledge transfer and creative audit of your current ads. Day 3–5: first batch of 10–15 creatives produced. Day 6–7: ads live in your accounts, learning phase starts. Week 2 onward: daily optimization, weekly reporting, continuous creative refresh.

Do I need to give you access to my ad accounts?

Yes, we request standard Business Manager / Ads Manager access — we never own the account. Your ad account, your data, your pixel. If we ever stop working together, you keep everything.

Which platforms do you run?

Meta, Google, Reddit, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Pinterest. We're a Reddit Certified Partner, so Reddit is often a high-leverage add-on for SaaS and DTC brands not already there. On the call we'll tell you the 1–2 platforms most likely to move your metric.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

Month-to-month. 30-day notice to cancel. No setup fees. The 45-day guarantee is the only commitment, and it's on us, not you.

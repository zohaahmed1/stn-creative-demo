Cookie Policy

Effective Date: September 11, 2025

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide a better user experience and analyze website performance.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies (Always Active)

  • Website functionality and navigation
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • User session management
  • Form submission processing

Analytics Cookies

  • Google Analytics for website performance
  • User behavior and traffic analysis
  • Conversion tracking and reporting
  • A/B testing and optimization

Marketing Cookies (With Consent)

Our Lead Generation Campaigns

  • Facebook/Meta Pixel: Track quiz completions and lead form submissions
  • Google Ads Tracking: Monitor assessment page visits and conversions
  • TikTok Pixel: Measure engagement with our video ads and landing pages
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag: B2B lead generation and retargeting
  • Custom Audiences: Create lookalike audiences based on quiz participants
  • Retargeting Pixels: Show follow-up ads to quiz takers and website visitors

Client Campaign Management

  • Conversion tracking for client advertisements
  • Audience building for client retargeting campaigns
  • Performance measurement across all platforms

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can control cookies through:

  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow cookie management
  • Cookie Consent Banner: Available on first website visit
  • Privacy Settings: Contact us to update preferences

Note: Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality and our ability to provide personalized services.

Third-Party Cookies

We work with these third-party services that may set cookies:

  • Google (Analytics, Ads, Tag Manager)
  • Meta/Facebook (Pixel, Conversions API)
  • TikTok (Pixel, Events API)
  • LinkedIn (Insight Tag)

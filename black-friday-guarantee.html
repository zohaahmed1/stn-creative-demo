Black Friday Ads Guarantee

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The short version. If our 18 videos aren't at least 20% cheaper per purchase than your current ads over the same 30 days, we'll refund 50% of what you paid. You keep the ads.

1. What's covered

  • This guarantee applies to the Black Friday AI UGC ad pack: 6 AI UGC video ads with 3 hooks each (18 videos) for a one-time fee of $1,500 USD, or $1,200 USD with the 20% discount for booking and attending a call with us.
  • It covers the fee you paid us. It doesn't cover your ad spend or any other costs.
  • One guarantee per brand.

2. The promise

Within 30 days of launch, our 18 videos together will cost at least 20% less per purchase than your comparison ads over the same dates. If they don't, we'll refund 50% of the fee you paid.

For example, if your comparison ads cost $50 per purchase during the test, our ads need to come in at $40 or less.

3. Timeline

  1. You pay the fee in full before we start.
  2. We deliver the 18 videos within 7 days of receiving your payment and everything we need: product links, brand assets and a few past ads that did well. If something's missing, the 7 days start when it arrives.
  3. Within 7 days of delivery, we set up the test in your ad account and you approve the budget. The test starts on the first day our ads spend money.
  4. The test runs for 30 days.

4. How the test is set up

We set up the test for you, and both sides are set up the same way, so the test only compares the ads.

  • Access. You give us access to create campaigns in your Meta ad account. Nothing goes live until you approve the daily budget.
  • Our side. Our 18 videos run together in their own ad set.
  • Your side. Your 6 ads that spent the most in the 30 days before the test run together in a second ad set.
  • Same setup. Both ad sets use the same targeting, the same purchase goal, the same daily budget and the same attribution setting. We use Meta's built-in A/B test where we can.
  • Enough spend. Each ad set spends at least $1,500 USD during the test.
  • Same offer. Both sides promote the same products, prices and discounts, and send people to the same store.
  • No changes mid-test. Don't edit, pause or add ads on either side during the test unless we agree in writing.
  • Working tracking. Your Meta pixel or Conversions API has to track purchases correctly for the whole test. If tracking breaks, the test is extended by the days it was down.
  • Rejected ads. If Meta rejects one of our ads because of something in our creative, we'll replace it within 2 business days and the test carries on.

5. How we measure it

  • We use Ads Manager. For each ad set, cost per purchase is the amount spent divided by purchases over the test dates, with the same attribution setting on both sides.
  • We read the results in your own Ads Manager, so you see the same numbers we do.

6. How to claim

  1. Email sales@skipthenoisemedia.com within 14 days after the test ends.
  2. We'll check the numbers within 5 business days.
  3. If the guarantee applies, we'll refund 50% of the fee you paid to your original payment method within 14 days.
  4. You keep the ads either way.

7. When the guarantee doesn't apply

  • You didn't give us access or approve the budget within 7 days of delivery.
  • Either ad set spent less than the minimum.
  • Ads were edited, paused or added during the test without our written OK.
  • Purchase tracking wasn't working and wasn't fixed.
  • The ad account was restricted, or ads were rejected because of your product, your claims or your website.
  • The claim came in more than 14 days after the test ended.
  • You already used what you paid for the pack as a credit toward the monthly plan.

8. Going monthly

  • If you start our monthly plan (4 new AI UGC ads a week for $4,000 USD a month) within 30 days of getting your 18 videos, what you paid for the pack comes off your first month.
  • You can use the credit or this guarantee, not both. If you take the credit, the 50% refund no longer applies to the pack.

Fees for this offer are in US dollars. Everything else follows our Terms of Service.

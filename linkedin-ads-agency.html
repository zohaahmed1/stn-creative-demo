Where it earns its cost

Four situations where LinkedIn is the right answer.

And one where it is not, which we say more often than most agencies would.

Named account targeting

If you sell into a defined list of companies, matched audiences against your CRM and target account list is hard to replicate anywhere else.

Buying committees, not buyers

Enterprise deals get decided by several people with different jobs. This is the only channel where you can reach all of them deliberately.

High ACV, long cycle

When one closed deal pays for a quarter of media, a high cost per lead stops mattering. Below that threshold it matters a great deal.

When we say use Reddit instead

For developer tools, security and infrastructure, CPCs on Reddit routinely land well below LinkedIn for people actively researching rather than passively scrolling. This is the recommendation we make most often.

Is this a fit

We're not for everyone. Here's the honest version.

You're a good fit if

  • You are B2B SaaS with a defined ICP and an ACV that carries the cost
  • You sell into named accounts
  • You want senior people doing the actual work, not juniors behind an account manager
  • You are willing to test properly — winners get more budget, losers get cut in under two weeks
  • You can tell a qualified lead from a lead in your CRM

We're probably not your agency if

  • Your product is under a few thousand a year
  • You want cheap leads — this is the wrong channel by definition
  • You have not worked out what a qualified lead is worth to you
  • You need SEO, email and content too. Paid media and the creative behind it is what we do

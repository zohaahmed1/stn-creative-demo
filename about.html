About Skip the Noise Media

Performance creative agency · Toronto, Canada

Skip the Noise Media is a performance creative agency based in Toronto, Canada. We make ad creative with AI and run the paid media behind it for ecommerce, DTC and SaaS brands in the US and Canada. The agency was founded by Zoha Ahmed and Sonia Mohapatra, who both came from WPP and GroupM. Our team has managed over $100M in ad spend.

What we do

We make the ads and we run the media. Our team plans the angles and directs every ad, and AI handles the production, so brands get far more new creative each month than a traditional shoot allows. That includes UGC video ads, statics and motion, sized for each platform.

We buy media on Meta, TikTok, Reddit, Google and LinkedIn. Reddit is where we have the most depth, as a Reddit Certified Partner that runs both Reddit ads and organic Reddit work.

We work with ecommerce and DTC brands, and with B2B and B2C SaaS companies, usually spending $10,000 or more a month on ads. See our services or look at the ads we make.

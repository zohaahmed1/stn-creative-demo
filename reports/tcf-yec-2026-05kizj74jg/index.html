How these numbers are counted
  • Figures run from Nov 1, 2026 to today and refresh each time the page is opened.
  • Spend is shown in US dollars. Accounts billed in Canadian dollars are converted at 1 USD = 1.3766 CAD.
  • Donations are the gifts each ad platform credits to its ads, including people who saw an ad and donated later. One gift can be credited by more than one platform.
  • LinkedIn reports donations together with other website actions, so its donations are not added to the totals.
  • X (Twitter) results are reported separately.

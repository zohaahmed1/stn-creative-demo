Google Ads Agency

Google Ads, with the search terms actually read.

Google is the only channel where people tell you exactly what they want before you spend anything. Most accounts waste that by never reading the search terms report and letting Performance Max spend wherever it likes.

Ex-GroupM & WPP team·$50M+ media managed·Reddit Certified Partner

The short version. Google captures demand that already exists. It does not create it. That makes it the most reliable channel in a mix and the easiest one to quietly waste money on, because bad spend looks like good spend until you open the search terms.

What goes wrong in most accounts

Nobody reads the search terms. This is the single highest-return hour in paid search and it goes undone for months. Broad match plus smart bidding will find you traffic, and a meaningful share of it will be queries you would never have chosen. The report tells you exactly which, and negatives are free.

Branded and non-branded get reported together. If your branded campaigns sit in the same number as everything else, your blended cost per acquisition looks great and tells you nothing. People searching your name were going to find you. Separate them or you cannot see what the account is actually doing.

Performance Max runs unsupervised. PMax works, and it works better with guardrails. Left alone it will spend into Display and brand queries because those convert cheaply, then report a healthy CPA built on demand you already had.

Conversion tracking counts the wrong things. Newsletter signups and demo requests weighted the same, form views counted as conversions, offline closes never fed back. The bidding optimises toward whatever you told it mattered, so if that is wrong everything downstream is wrong.

How we run it

Search terms weekly, negatives always. Unglamorous, and it is where the money is.

Branded split out from day one. So you can see the real cost of new demand rather than an average that hides it.

PMax with structure. Asset groups built around real product or ICP segments, brand exclusions on, and Search running properly alongside rather than being absorbed.

Tracking before spend. Conversion actions that reflect what you actually want, values attached where they exist, and offline conversion imports where the deal closes outside the browser.

Creative and landing pages in the same hands. Most PPC shops hand you a campaign and a report and leave the page alone. Message match between ad and page is half of what decides conversion rate, and it is hard to fix when two suppliers own the two halves.

Search, Shopping and PMax

Search is intent capture. Highest quality traffic in the mix, and the place competitor and high-intent product queries live. For SaaS it usually carries the account.

Shopping is where eCommerce spend belongs, and it is decided by the feed more than the bids. Titles, attributes and image quality do more for performance than most bid adjustments.

Performance Max is efficient when it has clean inputs and dangerous when it does not. Good assets, real segmentation and brand exclusions turn it into a scaling tool rather than a black box.

Google against the rest of the mix

Google answers demand. Reddit, Meta and TikTok create it. Accounts that run only Google plateau when they exhaust the existing search volume, which arrives sooner than people expect in narrow categories.

The pattern we see most often is Google carrying reliable, capped volume while a second channel does the work of making more people search in the first place. For technical B2B that second channel is usually Reddit, where CPCs run well below what Google charges for competitive SaaS keywords.

Who this is for

Good fit. eCommerce with real SKU depth and a working feed. SaaS with genuine search volume around the problem you solve. Brands paying for clicks they never examine, where the first month usually pays for itself in waste removed.

Not a fit. Categories nobody searches for yet, where you need demand creation before demand capture. Budgets under $2,000 a month, where there is not enough data for smart bidding to work with. Anyone wanting Google run in isolation from the creative and the landing page.

Results

We run Google alongside Meta and Reddit across DTC and SaaS accounts, including multi-channel programmes for skincare and home retail. $50M+ in paid media managed by the team across WPP and GroupM.

Frequently asked questions

What is the first thing you fix on a Google account?

The search terms report, then the branded split. Broad match and smart bidding will find traffic you would never have chosen, and the report names it. Negatives cost nothing. After that, separating branded spend so your cost per acquisition reflects new demand rather than people who were already looking for you.

Is Performance Max worth running?

Yes, with guardrails. Left unsupervised it drifts into Display and brand queries because those convert cheaply, then reports a healthy number built on demand you already had. Real asset group segmentation and brand exclusions turn it into a scaling tool.

How much budget does Google need?

At least $2,000 a month for smart bidding to have enough conversion data to work with. Below that you are better off on manual control or a different channel entirely.

Should we run Google or Reddit for B2B SaaS?

Usually both, doing different jobs. Google captures people already searching for a solution. Reddit reaches people describing the problem before they know what to search for. Google plateaus when search volume runs out, which happens sooner than expected in narrow categories.

Do you handle landing pages too?

Yes. Message match between the ad and the page decides a large share of conversion rate, and it is hard to fix when one supplier owns the campaign and another owns the page.

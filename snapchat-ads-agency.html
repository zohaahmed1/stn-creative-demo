Snapchat Ads Agency

Snapchat ads, where your Gen Z buyer already is.

Most DTC brands with a young audience are not on Snapchat, which is exactly why it works. Thinner auction, cheaper reach, and a buyer who is there several times a day.

Ex-GroupM & WPP team·$50M+ media managed·Reddit Certified Partner

The short version. Snapchat is the channel most DTC brands skip, and skipping it is why the CPMs stay reasonable. If your customer is under 30 and you are only on Meta, you are paying premium prices to reach a fraction of them.

The case for running it

We took on a fashion eCommerce brand with over 100,000 customers and a product that was already working on Meta. The problem was not performance, it was reach. Their Gen Z buyers were spending more time on TikTok and Snapchat than on Instagram, and the brand had no presence on either.

Meta alone could not deliver the frequency needed against an audience that was not primarily there. So we built platform-specific creative for both, rather than adapting what already worked.

Snapchat hit 4.3x ROAS inside four weeks of launch. TikTok reached the same figure, scaled up from an initial 3x. Combined managed spend across the two passed $17,000.

The reason that happened quickly is not that Snapchat is magic. It is that the auction is less crowded than Meta's for the same person, and the creative was built for the environment instead of ported into it.

What works on Snapchat

Full-screen vertical, or nothing. The format is not a placement option, it is the whole platform. Anything letterboxed reads as an ad that wandered in from somewhere else.

Fast, and then faster. Snapchat users move through content quicker than almost anywhere. The opening frame carries more weight here than on any other channel.

Creator-style over produced. Same principle as TikTok. Content that looks like it belongs between messages from friends performs better than content that looks like it came from a brand.

AR and try-on where the product suits it. Lenses are the one genuinely differentiated format Snapchat offers, and for beauty, eyewear and accessories they do something no static can.

Short cycles. Creative fatigue arrives fast, similar to TikTok. Plan production accordingly rather than expecting a winner to run for a quarter.

Snapchat next to TikTok

They overlap and they are not the same. TikTok is a discovery feed people open to be entertained. Snapchat is a messaging app with content attached, and people arrive there to talk to friends.

In practice that means creative travels between them better than it travels from Meta, but not perfectly. On the fashion account we ran both, and they reached the same ROAS by different routes. Running both is usually the right answer for a Gen Z DTC brand, and running neither while complaining about Meta costs is the common mistake.

Who this is for

Good fit. DTC and eCommerce with a customer base skewing under 30. Fashion, beauty, accessories and anything visual. Brands already at ceiling on Meta who need incremental reach rather than a rescue. Teams who can turn creative around quickly.

Not a fit. B2B, in every case. Products with an older buyer, where the audience simply is not there. Brands who cannot produce vertical video, because there is no version of this that works with repurposed square assets.

Where it sits in a mix

Meta stays the testing ground and usually the biggest line. TikTok and Snapchat are the incremental reach against a young audience, and they are cheaper per impression because fewer advertisers bother. Pinterest catches the same buyer earlier, while they are still planning. Reddit is the channel we are certified in and the one that works for research-stage buying rather than impulse.

Results

4.3x ROAS on Snapchat inside four weeks of launch for a fashion eComm brand. 4.3x on TikTok on the same account, scaled from 3x. $17,000+ managed across the two platforms. $50M+ in paid media managed by the team across WPP and GroupM.

Frequently asked questions

Is Snapchat worth running if we are already on Meta and TikTok?

Usually yes, if your buyer is under 30. The auction is thinner because fewer advertisers bother, so the same person often costs less to reach. On a fashion account we ran it hit 4.3x ROAS inside four weeks of launch.

Can we run our TikTok creative on Snapchat?

It travels better than Meta creative does, but not perfectly. Both reward vertical, fast, creator-style content, so the starting point is closer. Expect to adjust rather than copy.

How quickly can Snapchat work?

Faster than most channels when the creative is right. The fashion account above reached 4.3x inside four weeks. That is not typical of every account, but a thin auction means less time fighting for delivery.

Does Snapchat work for B2B?

No. We would rather tell you that than take the budget. For B2B, Reddit and LinkedIn are the channels worth your money.

What budget does a real test need?

$2,000 to $3,000 a month over 60 days, plus the ability to produce vertical video regularly. Creative fatigue arrives quickly here, so production capacity matters more than budget size.

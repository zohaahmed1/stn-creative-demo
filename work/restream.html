The problem

A leading streaming platform was spending $30–50k per week across paid channels and growing — but standard attribution couldn't tell them which channels were actually driving new subscribers versus just claiming credit. Branded search looked efficient on paper. In reality, it was consuming nearly half the paid media budget and had an actual CAC north of $600.

They needed two things: a measurement framework they could trust, and a creative strategy that could actually scale to new regions without blowing up CAC.

What we built

  • A triangulated measurement framework combining Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM), multi-touch attribution (MTA), and incrementality testing — integrated directly with their subscription and reactivation data for clean, reliable inputs
  • Incrementality tests to isolate the true impact of paid social on overall conversions, separate from what last-click attribution was reporting
  • Persona-level creative strategy for three audience segments — Creators, Coaches, and Marketing Leaders — with messaging tailored to each segment's core pain points: discoverability, monetization, and audience growth
  • A content strategy built around resources each segment actually wanted: webinars, creator monetization ebooks, and competitor comparison guides
  • Expansion into Europe, Canada, and Australia with region-specific creative and budget allocation

What the data revealed

The MMM surfaced something the client hadn't seen before: branded search had a CAC of over $600 and was taking up to 48% of the total paid media mix. Non-brand search and paid social were being systematically underutilized — they were more efficient, but getting a smaller share of budget.

We recommended cutting branded search spend and reallocating toward higher-efficiency channels. The results came quickly. Within weeks of the reallocation, CAC dropped below 2024 averages for the first time all year while new subscriber growth accelerated simultaneously.

Results

  • CAC dropped 19% after the branded search reallocation — YoY subscriber growth went from ~9% to 28% in the same period. Growth got bigger, not just more efficient.
  • New subscriber YoY growth rate hit its highest point in 70 weeks
  • Non-brand search acquisitions grew 57% YoY (2,561 → 4,010) while CAC fell from $430 to $327 — on 18% more spend
  • At $90k/week in spend, blended CAC landed in the low $60s — a level that typically produces CACs in the $80s
  • Paid social incrementality test confirmed a 6% relative lift in search demand driven by paid social (p-value 0.004, R² 0.99) — proving cross-channel halo effects that last-click attribution had been missing entirely

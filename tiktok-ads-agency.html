TikTok Ads Agency

TikTok ads, built for TikTok.

The fastest way to waste money on TikTok is to run your Meta creative on it. Different platform, different grammar, different reason people are there. We produce for the feed it is going into.

Ex-GroupM & WPP team·$50M+ media managed·Reddit Certified Partner

The short version. TikTok has the cheapest attention of any major paid channel and the shortest creative lifespan. Both of those facts come from the same place: the feed moves fast and the algorithm is ruthless about what it distributes. That rewards brands who can produce constantly and punishes brands who cannot.

Why repurposed Meta creative fails here

This is the single most common mistake and it is expensive. A polished brand asset that performs on Instagram reads as an interruption on TikTok, because TikTok users are not scrolling a feed of their friends with ads mixed in. They are scrolling a feed of entertainment, and anything that looks like advertising gets skipped before the first second is over.

When we took on a fashion eComm brand that was already doing well on Meta, the first decision was the most important one: do not adapt the Meta creative. Build for the platform. That brand had over 100,000 customers and a proven product, and its Gen Z audience was on TikTok and Snapchat while the brand was on neither.

Platform-native creative took that account to 4.3x ROAS on TikTok, scaled from an initial 3x, across a budget that grew past $17,000 in managed spend on TikTok and Snapchat combined.

What actually works on TikTok

Sound on, always. TikTok is an audio-first platform in a way no other channel is. Creative built to work muted is creative built for the wrong place.

The first second, not the first three. Whatever is on screen when the video starts decides everything. Not a logo, not a title card. Something happening.

Creator-style over studio. A single person talking to a phone camera reliably beats a produced spot, because it matches what surrounds it in the feed. That is a format decision, not a quality decision.

Volume, because burn rate is brutal. A winning TikTok asset has a much shorter useful life than a winning Meta asset. Frequency builds faster and fatigue arrives sooner. Whatever creative cadence you run on Meta, TikTok needs more.

Trends are a format, not a strategy. Borrowing a trending sound or structure is a useful way to feel native. Building your account on chasing them is not a plan, because by the time a trend is legible it is usually over.

Our TikTok process

The Signal Method, same four steps we run on every channel. Volume in, signal out.

Days 1–2, baseline. Account history, competitor creative on the platform, and what your audience is already watching in your category.

Days 3–5, volume. First creative batch, built for TikTok from the brief rather than exported from another channel.

Days 6–7, isolate. Live with a real test structure, baseline inside 72 hours.

Week 2 onward, amplify. Scale what works, cut what does not, produce the next batch off what you just learned. On TikTok this cycle runs faster than anywhere else.

Who TikTok is for

Good fit. DTC and eCommerce with visual products and a younger buyer. Brands already working on Meta who need a second channel rather than a rescue. Anyone who can approve creative quickly, because the cycle is fast and a slow approval chain wastes the advantage.

Not a fit. Considered B2B purchases with long sales cycles and small buying committees. Products that need a paragraph of explanation before they make sense. Brands who want three assets a quarter, where the burn rate will beat you.

TikTok versus the other channels we run

Meta is the better creative testing environment and the deeper targeting pool. If you run one channel, it is usually that one.

TikTok has lower CPMs and a faster learning loop, at the cost of a much shorter creative lifespan. Strong for DTC, hard for B2B.

Snapchat sits close to TikTok on audience and format and often runs alongside it. On the fashion account above, both hit 4.3x.

Reddit reaches people researching rather than scrolling. The channel we are certified in, and the one where creative has to read as a contribution rather than an ad.

Frequently asked questions

Can you reuse our Meta creative on TikTok?

You can, and it usually underperforms. TikTok users are in a feed of entertainment rather than a feed of friends, so anything that reads as advertising gets skipped inside a second. We build for the platform from the brief instead of exporting from another channel.

How much budget does TikTok need?

Plan for $3,000 to $5,000 a month over 60 days. TikTok needs enough conversion volume to optimise and enough creative in rotation to survive the burn rate, and below that you get neither.

How fast does TikTok creative burn out?

Faster than Meta. Frequency builds quickly and fatigue arrives sooner, so whatever production cadence works for you on Meta, TikTok needs more. That is the main operational difference between the two channels.

Does TikTok work for B2B?

Rarely, and we will tell you so. Considered purchases with long cycles and small buying committees are better served by Reddit or LinkedIn. TikTok earns its place on visual products with a broad consumer audience.

Do we need to appear on camera?

No. Creator-style footage performs well but it does not have to be you, and plenty of strong assets use product, hands and voiceover with no presenter at all.

Talk to us about your account

We will look at what is running, what it is earning, and where the creative is costing you money. No deck. If TikTok is not the right channel for you we will say so, which happens more often than you would expect from an agency.
